# VICE CITY DUO

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01)
[![make.fun rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/Ahmadmukafi/gta6-cover-art-01-r5/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01)
[![make.fun reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/Ahmadmukafi/gta6-cover-art-01-r5/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01)

An offline web app that produces a finished GTA VI key-art poster and lets
you restyle it, fully offline in a desktop browser. Entry for the
[$250 GTA 6 Cover Art bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).

The hero is one rich, hand-drawn inline-SVG Vice City dusk scene,
full-bleed: a neon Ocean Drive skyline with lit windows and neon signs,
palm silhouettes, a low sports car with its headlights on, wet-street
reflections, stars and a big low sun — and two protagonist silhouettes,
Lucia and Jason back to back, split by the classic GTA diagonal.

- **Duo split** — drag the diagonal divider to shift how much of each
  protagonist's half shows; the split line and both silhouettes move live.
- **GTA-fy grade** — one slider from clean illustration to full GTA
  treatment (posterized colors, halftone dots, neon rim light); it drives
  both the staged preview and the PNG export.
- **3 looks** — Sunset Strip, Neon Night, Noir Vice.
- **Title + tagline editing**, drawn onto the poster live.
- **PNG export** at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080.

Everything is the entrant's own — no Rockstar Games logo, no official
artwork. Run it offline with any static server over `app/`, e.g.
`python3 -m http.server` from inside `app/`, then open
http://localhost:8000.
